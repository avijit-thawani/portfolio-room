# Varanasi rooftop v3

Canonical prompt for the nine-object composition. Style clauses can change, but
the geometry and negatives should remain stable until the layout is selected.

## Composition

Wide straight-on rooftop stage set, camera slightly elevated, natural
perspective, no lens distortion. Low sunset at upper right; every shadow falls
long toward the left. Broad weathered hostel terrace with an ochre parapet,
subtle stains, exposed pipe joints and generous empty concrete around every
object.

Three distinct depth bands:

1. **Background:** Ganges and layered ghats on the left; modest riverside
   buildings and tiny boats. Near center-right, the white marble New Vishwanath
   Temple of BHU, its recognizable shikhara 12–15% of frame height above a green
   campus canopy. One small black water tank only on a distant neighboring roof.
   Two or three tiny kites.
2. **Rear/midground:** camera, large machine, green chalkboard, project desk and
   globe, staggered rather than lined up.
3. **Foreground:** charpai and open-case typewriter.

## Nine targets

- **Reading:** woven charpai front-left, angled toward the river, one tidy stack
  of colorful blank-spined books.
- **Film:** vintage black cinema camera on a tall wooden tripod in the back-left
  corner, behind the charpai and aimed at the ghats.
- **Making:** enormous freestanding Rube Goldberg machine at center-left,
  directly on a floor frame, chest-high and one-quarter frame width. Large brass
  gears, marble helix, ramps, pendulum, counterweights and copper pipes. No
  supporting table.
- **Research:** large dark-green chalkboard on wooden legs at back-center.
  Coherent symbolic chalk diagrams: token blocks splitting into subwords,
  embedding-vector columns, matrix cells, attention arcs and a number-line
  curve. No prose or readable labels.
- **Build:** substantial teak project desk center-right, open laptop on top,
  screen showing three abstract project tiles without words.
- **Affiliations:** four oversized separated lanyards hanging from the desk
  apron. Clean blank badges with color bars suggesting Microsoft multicolor,
  Amazon orange-black, USC cardinal-gold and IIT BHU burgundy-cream. Composite
  real logos later; do not generate them.
- **Writing:** portable vintage typewriter in its open case on a low wooden crate
  in the near-center foreground. No chair and no written page.
- **On stage:** compact wooden stool near the right, handheld microphone resting
  across its seat, one tidy cable coil below. No mask or standing microphone.
- **Travel:** large terrestrial globe on a tall brass floor stand at far-right,
  recognizable unlabeled continents, isolated from desk and stool.

All targets stay within the middle 70% of image height. Only the badge lanyards
may touch the project desk. The machine is the visual centerpiece; chalkboard,
globe, temple and camera provide separated vertical accents.

## Style selected for the first pass

Luminous hand-painted anime-background illustration: soft gouache texture, warm
muted colors, clean flat shading, crisp readable shapes, delicate atmospheric
haze, honey-gold light, river blue-grey, forest green, teak, brass and gulmohar
red. No photographic noise or depth-of-field blur.

## Strict negatives

No people, hands, portrait, lone chair, theatre mask, telescope, foreground
water tank, laptop on floor, small tabletop contraption, airplane, words,
lettering, readable text, numerals, generated logos, signs, watermark,
contradictory shadows, warped architecture, merged silhouettes or clutter.
