- Confine the Ganges and one irregular ghat cluster to the far-left quarter.
  The ghat tapers away quickly; it does not repeat across the horizon.
- Use open river behind the camera and machine, with tiny boats at varied angles.
- Fill the broad center with a simple, layered canopy of mature BHU-campus trees.
  Add only a few muted gulmohar crowns and almost no visible architecture.
- Place the white New Vishwanath Temple slightly right of center, isolated above
  the grove, recognizable at 10–12% of frame height with green breathing room.
- A tiny black tank may sit on an almost-hidden distant roof at extreme right.

The detailed foreground must read against foliage, not a city wall. Preserve
charpai perspective, closed laptop, tangled badges, right-side sunset and all
leftward shadows. Do not add repeated steps, cloned facades or buildings behind
