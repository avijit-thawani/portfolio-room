# NYC rooftop v2 — balanced arc

Keep the original bright stylized-3D daytime look: pale concrete, clear blue sky,
matte materials, straight glass towers and short forward-right shadows.

Use fewer, distinct high-rises with clean window grids and air between
silhouettes. Arrange the content at staggered depths around a calm central path:

- back-left cinema camera;
- front-left low cedar rooftop bench with four blank books, open laptop with a
  matte-black powered-off screen, and four tangled affiliation lanyards;
- mid-left compact freestanding brass contraption on a floor base;
- back-center-right green NLP chalkboard;
- center open-case typewriter directly on concrete;
- right-center stool with handheld mic and cable;
- far-right unlabeled terrestrial globe.

No desk, corkboard, mask, telescope, laptop on the ground, screen content,
orderly badges, water tanks, warped towers, repeated skyscrapers, readable text,
logos or clutter. Collapse the skyline onto the far depth plane; flatten the
chalkboard and laptop screen before posterizing.
