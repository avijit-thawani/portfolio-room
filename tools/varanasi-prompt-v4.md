# Varanasi rooftop v4 — lived-in layout

Corrections after v3:

- Generate from imagination, **without the old scene as a reference**.
- The river is mostly open water. Use one compact, irregular ghat area followed
  by gaps, trees, courtyards and buildings with different setbacks. Never repeat
  a staircase/facade module.
- Charpai long rails run parallel to the parapet and share its vanishing points.
  Show it from the long side with four grounded legs and plausible rope weave.
- Laptop is closed, dark and unlit.
- Affiliation badges are an accidental tangle, not a display: crossed ribbons,
  unequal lengths, overlapping crooked cards, one caught in a drawer.
- Favor natural wear and asymmetry over exhibition spacing.

## Scene

Wide Varanasi university-hostel rooftop at sunset, viewed diagonally from a
slightly elevated camera. One coherent perspective. Low sun at upper right, all
shadows left. Weathered ochre concrete, teak, brass, green chalkboard and
blue-grey river. Broad bare floor between the major objects.

Background: open Ganges on the left, one irregular short run of steps, varied
clusters of old houses and trees, small boats at different angles. The white New
Vishwanath Temple rises center-right above a campus canopy, prominent but
distant. A small black tank sits only on a neighboring roof. Sparse kites.

Objects:

1. Front-left charpai parallel to parapet; untidy books near one end.
2. Back-left cinema camera on tripod aimed toward river.
3. Center-left oversized freestanding Rube Goldberg machine on a floor frame.
4. Back-center broad green board with loose token, vector, matrix and attention
   diagrams but no readable labels.
5. Center-right scarred desk with a **closed** black laptop.
6. Four tangled affiliation lanyards draped over desk handles and corner.
7. Near-center open-case typewriter on a low crate, no paper or chair.
8. Right-midground stool with a handheld mic and loose cable coil.
9. Far-right unlabeled terrestrial globe on a brass floor stand.

No people, portrait, mask, telescope, foreground tank, open laptop, lit screen,
airplane, repeated ghats, cloned houses, readable text, numerals, logos,
watermark, warped charpai, floating furniture, straight-row layout or neat badge
spacing.
